Best Free Freedom App Alternative for Android in 2025

By TBTechs · May 2025 · 7 min read

Bottom line: Freedom costs $6.99/month ($84/year) and has significant weaknesses on Android specifically. FocusFlow is free, open source, and adds three enforcement layers Freedom lacks: Device Admin, Temptation Log, and content-specific blocking. For Android users, FocusFlow is the stronger choice at every price point.

Why People Look for a Freedom Alternative

  • $6.99/month is steep for a focus app
  • Freedom uses a VPN that can be bypassed by toggling airplane mode or disabling the VPN from Android quick settings
  • No Device Admin — Freedom can be uninstalled in seconds
  • No content-specific blocking — blocks entire apps, not just Reels or Shorts
  • No temptation log or weekly behavioural reports
  • The free tier allows only 7 sessions ever

Where Freedom Still Wins

If you need cross-platform blocking (Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android all in one subscription), Freedom is the only option that covers all four platforms simultaneously. FocusFlow is Android-only.

The Android Problem with Freedom's VPN

On Android, Freedom's VPN approach has a specific weakness: the Android VPN toggle is accessible from the quick settings shade without unlocking the phone. FocusFlow's local VPN, by contrast, is protected: attempts to disconnect it during a session trigger a violation alert and are logged in the Temptation Log.

How to Switch from Freedom to FocusFlow

  1. End your current Freedom session and cancel your Freedom subscription
  2. Download FocusFlow APK from GitHub Releases
  3. Install and run through the 5-minute setup (Accessibility Service + Usage Stats + Device Admin)
  4. Add the same apps you blocked in Freedom to your FocusFlow block list
  5. Optionally add content-specific blocks for Shorts/Reels
For Android users: FocusFlow is the better choice in every category except cross-platform reach. It's free, enforces harder, and doesn't collect your usage data.
