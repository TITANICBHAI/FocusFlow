Full feature list

Everything FocusFlow
can do for you

Block distractions, plan your day and understand your habits. Free, with no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

Block what pulls you away

Pick the style that fits your day

Use one mode or combine several. Everything below is free.

🎯

Focus on a task

Start a task, choose the apps you allow for it, and everything else is blocked until the task ends.

⏱️

Block right now, no task needed

Choose apps and an end time, or tap a quick preset. You can extend a block by 30 minutes, 1, 2 or 4 hours, but you can't cut it short.

🗓️

Quiet evenings on autopilot

Set repeating block windows, such as weeknights. You can also cut the internet for those apps during the window.

🔒

Always-on blocking

Keep the apps you never want to open blocked around the clock.

📏

Limit an app instead of banning it

Give an app a daily allowance: a number of opens, a time budget, or a cooldown between opens.

🔎

Keyword blocker

Block words that show up in web addresses, searches and on-screen text. When one appears, FocusFlow sends you back to the home screen.

Optional
📡

Cut internet for chosen apps

An on-device VPN can switch off internet access for the apps you pick. It stays on your phone; no traffic goes to a server.

🗑️

Delete the apps for good

Nuclear Mode lets you pick apps from your block list and uninstall them. Android asks you to confirm each one.

Block the feed, not the app

Keep the useful part of an app

Some apps are fine until one screen pulls you in. These tools remove just that screen.

Optional
📱

YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels

Turn on the Shorts or Reels blocker and FocusFlow steers you away from the short-video player. The rest of each app stays usable.

Beta
🎛️

Custom Screen Rules

Point at the exact screen you want gone, such as a shopping feed or a tab inside any app. Capture it once, test it, and switch it on from the Defense tab like any other protection. You can keep up to 30 rules.

Beta
🤝

Share rules with friends

Export your rules as a file or import someone else's. Anything invalid is refused with a reason, and Settings or FocusFlow itself can never be blocked by a rule.

🔐

No screenshots

FocusFlow never takes a screenshot. Screen rules read the on-screen elements an app shares with Android's accessibility service. It's beta because an app update can change a screen and break a rule.

Home Launcher

A calmer home screen

Use FocusFlow as your home screen, so the first thing you see isn't a wall of tempting icons.

Optional
🏠

A minimal home screen

Choose a Classic or Glassy look and pick which apps appear in the app drawer. Pin the apps you need and hide the ones you don't.

Optional
🖼️

Make it yours

Set your own wallpaper and a clock style so the launcher feels like your phone, not a lock screen.

Optional
🛑

Stays put during a block

During a standalone block the launcher can lock itself, and it hides Uninstall from the long-press menu, so switching away or removing it is harder in the moment.

Built to be hard to quit

Protection that only gets tighter

A block is only useful if it can't be quietly weakened halfway through. You choose how strict to be.

📈

Locks that only tighten

While a block runs you can add apps and keywords, but existing ones stay locked. Schedules can't be deleted until the block ends, and a standalone block can't be stopped early.

🔑

Two passwords

A Defense password guards your protections and a Focus session password guards ending a session. They need 8+ characters, there's a strength meter and a “Generate for me” button, and you can ask someone you trust to set them. There is no recovery, so write them down.

Optional
🧱

Protect system controls

During a block, FocusFlow can keep you out of the power menu, the notification shade and sensitive Settings pages, the usual shortcuts for undoing a block.

📲

Sideload Guard

If you install a new app during a protected session, FocusFlow notices and covers it with the block too.

Optional
🛡️

Device Admin

Adds resistance to force-stopping or uninstalling FocusFlow mid-session. It's an extra layer, not a guarantee.

🔄

Comes back after a restart

If your phone restarts, FocusFlow restores protection for you.

Optional
📳

A nudge you can feel

Optional and off by default: when a blocked app opens, the screen can dim, the phone can vibrate and a sound can play.

🆘

Emergencies are covered

The phone dialer and emergency apps stay reachable by default. If you truly must end a focus session, Emergency Override asks you to confirm and logs it. FocusFlow is a focus tool, so don't rely on it for emergency access.

See your habits

Know what you actually reach for

Everything here is worked out on your phone from Android's own usage data. Nothing is uploaded.

📊

Temptation Log and weekly report

FocusFlow records each time you try to open a blocked app (the latest 500). Every Sunday morning you get a weekly summary by app.

📈

Stats for today, this week and all time

Trend charts and a focus summary, plus an Extra screen with screen time, unlocks, sessions and quick checks.

🔍

Findings you can act on

FocusFlow spots patterns, like late-night use, a weekend spike, or an app that grabs your first minutes of the day, and explains them in plain words. Each finding has a one-tap action to block the app or cap its daily time.

Optional
🔔

Habit nudges

A gentle heads-up shortly before the hour you usually slip, with a short block ready to start. There's a daily limit, and you can switch them off any time.

🏆

Achievements and streaks

Earn badges for things like a focus session without opening a blocked app, or a 21-day streak.

Plan your day

A planner that works with the blocker

Tasks decide which apps are allowed, so planning and blocking stay in sync.

📝

Tasks and timeline

Add tasks quickly, choose the apps each one can use, and see your day on a timeline. Extend a task and the ones after it shift forward.

✅

Subtasks

Break a routine into steps with optional timers, sets and rest breaks, and photos. Handy for workouts and study plans.

Optional
🍅

Pomodoro cycles

Choose your own work and break lengths. Breaks unlock your apps, then blocking resumes.

⏰

Reminders that fit the task

Get a heads-up before a task starts, check-ins along the way and a warning before it ends, as a ringing alarm or a quiet notification.

🧩

Home-screen widget

See your current or next task, a countdown and progress at a glance. It shows nothing about your blocking setup.

🌙

Daily reflection

A short end-of-day check-in and day rating feed into your stats.

Make it yours

Plan with any AI assistant, back up anytime

FocusFlow never connects to an AI service. You stay in control of what gets imported.

🤖

Plan a week with an AI assistant

In Settings, tap Copy task-backup prompt, paste it into the assistant you already use, and describe your tasks. It writes a .focusflow file you open in FocusFlow. You get a review screen with Merge or Replace before anything is imported.

💾

Back up and restore

Save your setup as a .focusflow file and restore it when you need to, including on a new phone.

🎨

Your own block screen

Choose your own background image from your gallery and your own rotating quotes, or keep the built-in dark gradient and focus quotes.

🌗

Light and dark, your text size

Switch themes and adjust the text size to suit your eyes.

Works on the phone you have

Android 10 or newer, no root

Phone makers handle background apps in different ways, so FocusFlow includes help for the common ones.

📟

Free and open source

No ads, no subscription, no premium tier. The full source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 licence.

🛠️

Built-in troubleshooting

Step-by-step help for Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Realme/Oppo and stock Android, including what to do if the Accessibility toggle is greyed out.

🔏

Your data stays on your phone

Your setup and history are stored on your device. FocusFlow has no accounts and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Straight answers

What FocusFlow is not

  • Not tamper-proof. No Android app can stop a determined person who has full control of their own phone. FocusFlow is built to stop impulsive moments, and a password set with someone you trust makes it stronger.
  • Not a parental-control app. It's a self-control tool for your own phone, with no remote or multi-device management.
  • Android only, with no cloud sync between devices. The app is in English; this website is available in several languages.
  • Not on Google Play at the moment. Get it from GitHub Releases, Huawei AppGallery, Mobile Seva AppStore or Indus Appstore.

All of this. Completely free.
